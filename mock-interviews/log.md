## How to fill this in

- **Time limit** — what you gave yourself (e.g. 20 min, 30 min, 45 min for two problems).
- **Self-assessment** — be honest: Optimal / Working but not optimal / Didn't finish / Needed hints.
- **Notes for next time** — one concrete thing to improve (e.g. "state brute force out loud first," "review sliding window before next session," "practice explaining time complexity while coding").

## Progress tracking

Periodically skim this log and update [../cheatsheets/patterns.md](../cheatsheets/patterns.md)
or add new problems to `problems/` targeting patterns that keep showing up
as weak spots.
